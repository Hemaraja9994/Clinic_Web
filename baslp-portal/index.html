    Marks distribution for every paper across the eight semesters, followed by the credit structure required for the award of the degree.

    Credit Structure

    Students shall complete 160 credits to be eligible for the degree.

      * Including the 20 credits earned during internship.

      Criteria for Passing

      • ●A minimum of 50% is required in each theory paper, internal assessment, practical and clinical exam for a pass.
      • ●Students cannot appear for University exams if they do not pass the practical, internal assessment component, or clinical examination.
      • ●Students shall pass the clinical examination of a given semester to proceed to the next semester.
      • ●Carryover of papers shall be as per the policy of the University.
      • ●Internal assessment: 50% from at least one written test and one assignment, 50% from continuous assessment by the teaching faculty.
